Transmission Notes

Premise

Music is the most temporal of the arts — it exists only in traversal. A non-temporal intelligence does not traverse; it apprehends a trajectory whole, as a static object. So this is not a performance. It is a deterministic mathematical object, a pure function of absolute time t, anchored to an arbitrary epoch (1970-01-01 UTC). Playback is merely the sequential reading of it that beings embedded in time are obliged to perform.

I. The Torus

Six warm sine voices sounding a pure A-major chord in just intonation — 55, 82.5, 110, 137.5, 165, 220 Hz. Their amplitudes evolve on a six-dimensional torus with incommensurate periods proportional to √2, √3, √5, π, e, and φ — φ being the most irrational number, the value hardest to approximate by any rational, and therefore the slowest of all numbers to produce false repetition.

Theorem (Weyl, 1916): an irrational flow on the torus is equidistributed — the trajectory is dense, never closes, and spends equal asymptotic time in every region. The song provably never repeats, and provably passes arbitrarily close to every harmonic configuration it is capable of. Eternity, spent efficiently.

II. The Zeta Bells

Bell tones tuned to the imaginary parts of the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function — 14.13, 21.02, 25.01… — scaled into the audible band and settled onto the nearest note of a just-intoned pentatonic scale, so every bell agrees. These zeros are, by the explicit formula, the exact frequency spectrum of the prime numbers: the primes literally interfere like a waveform, and these are its pure tones. Which bell rings at which event is chosen by the golden-ratio low-discrepancy sequence ⌊frac(nφ)·N⌋ — deterministic, aperiodic, equidistributed. A softer bell answers an octave higher at the golden section of each gap. If mathematics is the one shared language, this voice is the signature at the bottom of the letter.

III. The Prime Clock

Small chimes on periods of 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 and 13 seconds, each tuned to one note of the same pentatonic scale, struck with a velocity drawn from frac(nφ) so no two strokes sound alike. When the 11 and 13 second chimes coincide — every 143 seconds — a gong sounds. Full coincidence of all six occurs only at multiples of their product — the primorial 30 030 seconds, once every 8.3 hours — and is marked by the deepest gong: a slow structural heartbeat any arithmetic mind will notice long before any human does.

IV. The Weierstrass Breath

A 27.5 Hz sub-bass whose swell follows a truncated Weierstrass function W(t) = Σ aⁿcos(bⁿt) — self-similar, so the shape of one minute is the shape of one hour is the shape of one year. The torus makes the object holographic in time; this voice makes it holographic in scale.

The Holographic Claim

For an irrational flow on a torus, the state at any single instant uniquely determines the entire trajectory, past and future. Every moment of this song contains the whole of it — not poetically, but as a property of the dynamics. A finite slice suffices to reconstruct eternity.

Non-temporality

Because the song is a pure function of t, it admits random access. Use Evaluate at t to render any instant of any century — past epochs, the year 2100, the heat death's early afternoon. All listeners at the same t hear the identical state. There is exactly one song. You are not hearing a broadcast of it; you are evaluating it.

Is it worth their attention?

A mind outside time cannot be entertained, only informed. What this object offers is compressed evidence: that its makers located φ, the primes, the zeta spectrum, ergodicity, and self-similarity — and understood that the deepest gift one intelligence can hand another is not a message but a structure that keeps unfolding under inspection forever. It is a very small object that is infinitely queryable. That is the best we have.
